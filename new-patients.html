Who We Are

Established 1971

Longmeadow Animal Hospital was established in its present location at 19764 Longmeadow Road, Hagerstown, MD in 1971. Since then, we have offered the highest quality veterinary medical care to the greater Hagerstown, Smithsburg, and Leitersburg area. We are dedicated to helping our companion animal friends live longer, healthier, and happier lives.

Longmeadow Animal Hospital offers complete veterinary care to dogs and cats. At this time, Longmeadow Animal Hospital does not offer care for reptiles or avians. Our goal is to improve the quality of your pet's life, and health.

Good to Know

Policies & Practical Info

  • Payment: Payment in full is due at the time of service; we're happy to provide an estimate prior to diagnostics and treatment. We accept cash, Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and CareCredit.
  • Leashes & carriers: All dogs must be leashed at all times in the waiting room; all cats should arrive in a secure carrier or on a leash.
  • Heartworm testing: Puppies get their first heartworm test at approximately 7 months. Dogs over 18 months need a current negative heartworm test (within the past 12 months) to be dispensed heartworm medication.
  • Medication refills: Please allow 24–48 hours' notice for all medication refill requests, including heartworm and flea/tick prevention.
  • Pill cutting: Optional $6 flat fee if you'd like our staff to cut your pet's pills; pill cutters available for purchase for $4.
